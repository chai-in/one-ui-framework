# Contributor Covenant code of conduct

We pledge to make participation in One UI a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion or sexual orientation.

## Community standards

Use welcoming language, give and receive constructive feedback, respect
differing experiences and take responsibility when your actions cause harm.
Sexualized attention, trolling, personal attacks, threats, harassment and
publishing someone's private information without permission are unacceptable.

These standards apply in project spaces and when someone publicly represents
the project, including at events and on social media.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report an incident privately to an uninvolved maintainer. If you need a
private contact, ask the repository owner for a confidential conversation
without posting incident details. For abuse on GitHub, the **Report content**
menu also offers a route to GitHub Support.

Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, protect the reporter's
privacy and share identifying details only as needed to address the incident.
They may remove or edit contributions that violate these standards and will
explain moderation decisions to the people affected when appropriate.

Consequences reflect the impact and pattern of conduct.

1. **Correction.** A private written explanation identifies the harm and the
   change expected. An apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A formal warning sets conditions for continued participation,
   which may include a period of no contact with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban.** Serious or sustained misconduct can lead to exclusion
   for a defined period, including restrictions on private contact.
4. **Permanent ban.** Repeated violations, harassment or aggression toward a
   person or group can lead to permanent exclusion from project spaces.
